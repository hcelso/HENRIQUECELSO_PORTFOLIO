3D Painting Texturing Look Development

Every surface painted by hand.

This page gathers four texturing projects made across different clients and formats. What connects them is the method: no scanned materials, no procedural shortcuts as a finish. Every color, wear mark and highlight was painted directly on the model in Substance Painter.

Hand painting is what allows a stylized asset to hold a defined visual language. The texture carries the drawing, not just the surface information.

The projects range from collectible pieces for RTFKT to avatars and environment kits for the game Nifty Island, plus a stylized character and a set of creatures.

Each set had its own constraints — real-time budgets, modular reuse, or a single hero render — and the painting approach was adjusted to each one.

Software — Substance Painter, Photoshop

Pods

rtfkt

Collectible pods textured as painted objects: graffiti, spray marks, illustrated panels and worn edges applied over a hard-surface model. The paint carries the character; the geometry stays clean.

Avatars

nifty island

A series of playable characters textured for the game. Each one had to read clearly at gameplay distance and hold the identity of its collection, working inside a real-time texture budget.

nifty island — character texturing

Houses & props

nifty island

Buildings, modular kits, furniture and props for the environments. Painted as families rather than single objects: the wood, fabric and paint had to stay consistent across dozens of pieces so players could combine them freely.

houses — texture painting and turntables

The same kit before painting. Everything that reads as material in the final asset — grain, joints, dirt, colored accents — was added in the texture.

props and modular kits — environment assets

Stylized character

art test

A single character taken further as a painting exercise: skin, hair, fabric and leather built up in layers, with the light and volume painted into the diffuse rather than left to the shader.

stylized character — render and turntables

Fish set

creature painting

A family of creatures painted with a shared palette and a loose, wet-brush treatment. Each one is distinct, but the color logic and the way the paint bleeds across the body keeps the set reading as one species.

fish set — creature texture painting

inside the file

Painted in layers, on the model.

Every asset is built as a stack of hand-painted layers and masks in Substance Painter, checked against the UV layout as the paint goes down. Working this way keeps the result editable and keeps the texture consistent when the same material has to appear across an entire set.

substance painter — layer stack and uv layout

one creative practice

Texturing as a drawing discipline.

Hand painting textures is where the graphic design side of my practice meets 3D. The decisions are the same ones a drawing asks for — palette, contrast, where the eye should land — applied to a surface that has to hold up from every angle and inside a real engine.
