# HENRIQUE CELSO — PROFESSIONAL PROFILE

## Professional Positioning

Henrique Celso is a **Creative / Visual Designer** and multidisciplinary creative professional with a background in graphic design and experience across multiple areas of visual communication.

Rather than being positioned as a specialist in a single discipline, his professional identity is based on the ability to combine different creative disciplines according to the needs of each project.

**Core positioning:**

> A multidisciplinary creative working across design, motion, image and 3D — turning ideas into visual experiences.

The goal is to communicate versatility as a strength, avoiding the perception of being a professional who simply "does a little bit of everything."

---

## Professional Background

Henrique started his career in **2013 as a Graphic Designer**.

Throughout his career, his practice expanded progressively into:

* Graphic Design
* Motion Design
* Illustration
* Video Editing
* Compositing
* Visual Development
* 3D Painting
* 3D Texturing

This evolution represents an expansion of his visual toolkit rather than a series of unrelated career changes.

His professional approach is therefore multidisciplinary: he can move between concept, design, image creation, animation, editing and final delivery depending on the requirements of a project.

## Portfolio Strategy

The portfolio should present Henrique primarily as a **Creative / Visual Designer**, rather than separating his identity into Graphic Designer, Motion Designer, Illustrator, Editor and 3D Artist.

The portfolio should be **project-driven rather than discipline-driven**.

Instead of organizing the main portfolio into:

* Graphic Design
* Motion
* Illustration
* Editing
* 3D

projects should be presented as complete creative works, with the disciplines listed as supporting information.

For example:

**PROJECT NAME**

Role: Creative / Visual Designer

Disciplines: Design / Motion / 3D / Editing

This communicates multidisciplinary capability without making the portfolio feel unfocused.

## Visual Direction

The desired portfolio aesthetic is **premium, minimal, editorial and highly visual**.

A major reference is Apple's product presentation philosophy.

The goal is not to copy Apple's visual identity, but to adopt principles such as:

* Strong visual hierarchy
* Large-scale imagery
* Generous negative space
* Minimal copy
* Precise typography
* Controlled animation
* Clear information hierarchy
* Progressive disclosure of information
* Strong transitions between sections
* High-quality presentation of the work

The site should feel **premium through precision and restraint**, not through excessive effects.

Avoid:

* Excessive particles
* Overly complicated navigation
* Gratuitous 3D effects
* "Website as a game" concepts
* Excessive text
* Generic designer portfolio templates

The technology and animation should support the work rather than become the main attraction.
