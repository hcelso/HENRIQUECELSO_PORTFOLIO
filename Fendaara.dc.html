A broken signal, designed on purpose.

Fendaara is an Instagram web series created at Greydot Lab in 2025. The brief for the opening was specific: a loading screen for a clandestine, improvised radio transmission.

That premise pointed the whole piece toward noise. Halftone patterns, glitches and chromatic aberration were not applied as a finishing effect — they became the material the opening is made of.

The universe of the series has a strong identity of its own, mixing futurism with elements from the original peoples of the planet where the story takes place.

I worked across the full sequence: concept and illustration, vectorization of the artwork, animation of every element on screen, and the glitch treatment that gives the piece its texture.

visual research

Futurism built on ancestral form.

The first moodboard set the visual territory: geometry, ornament and pattern from the original peoples of the planet, read through a technological lens. It defined the palette, the line language and the level of detail the screen would carry.

from sketch to vector

Drawing the screen before animating it.

concept — original illustration

vectorized artwork — final layout

The concept illustration established the composition and the ornamental system around the central loading state.

Vectorizing it was what made the animation possible: every circuit line, marker and counter became an independent element, ready to move on its own.

The design was built to hold noise. Precise, symmetrical and graphic enough that the distortion applied later would read as interference rather than as a mistake.

animation references

Glitch as a language, not an accident.

With the artwork finished, a second moodboard was assembled for the animation. The main reference was the glitch treatment in Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse and other productions directed by Alberto Mielgo.

motion moodboard — glitch references

phase 01

Every element on screen given life.

The first pass animated the artwork straight: circuits pulsing, counters running, the central loading state breathing. Clean, before any interference.

phase 01 — element animation

phase 02

Halftones, chromatic aberration, interference.

The second phase built the glitch treatment in After Effects — channel separation, halftone breakup and signal drop-outs timed against the animation underneath.

phase 02 — glitch and chromatic aberration

phase 03

Drawn by hand, one frame at a time.

The last phase added illustrated elements animated frame by frame over the composition. They reinforce the digital glitches with something irregular and hand-made, and give the piece the personality the software effects alone could not reach.

phase 03 — frame by frame illustration

final piece

Delivered vertical, for the feed.

The series lives on Instagram, so the opening was delivered in vertical format — the version that actually plays before each episode.

vertical cut — instagram

one creative practice

Concept, illustration and motion in a single piece.

On Fendaara, the same hands took the opening from reference gathering to illustration, vectorization, animation, compositing and final delivery. Working across disciplines is what allowed the noise to be designed rather than added: the drawing was built for the glitch, and the glitch was drawn back into the piece.
